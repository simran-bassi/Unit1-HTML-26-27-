S'mores Cookies

I picked this recipe because I love cookies and s'mores! Every year, my family goes on a camping trip and we always make these delicious smores, and I wanted to replicate that in the form of cookies for an easier eating experience.

  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons hot water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 pack of graham crackers
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • 1 cup large marshmallows

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Mix together the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar until smooth. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla. Dissolve baking soda in hot water. Add to batter along with salt. Stir in flour, chocolate chips, and marshmallows. Drop by large spoonfuls onto ungreased pans.
  3. Place a graham cracker and large marshmallow on baking pan for each cookie. Then, cover them with the cookie dough.
  4. Bake for about 20 minutes in the preheated oven, or until edges are nicely browned.
  5. Once cookies are done, remove from oven and immediately press graham cracker pieces into the tops of the cookies.

Notes

  • Equipment needed:
    • Mixing bowls
    • Measuring cups
    • Spoons
    • Baking pan
  • Substitutions:
    • Replace white sugar with brown sugar for a more intense flavor.
    • Use dark chocolate chips instead of semisweet for a richer taste.
